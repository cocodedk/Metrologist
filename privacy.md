# Privacy Policy — Metrologist

**App:** Metrologist (`com.cocode.measureapp`)
**Developer:** CoCode.dk — Babak Bandpey
**Last updated:** 7 October 2026

> The canonical, always-current version of this policy is published at
> **https://measure.cocode.dk/privacy/**

**Metrologist does not collect personal data, and it does not upload your photos or measurements by itself.**
It is an Android app that measures flat surfaces from a photo, and every calculation runs entirely on
your device. A picture leaves the app only when you tap Export and choose where it goes.

## Camera

Metrologist uses your device camera for one purpose: to photograph the surface you want to measure,
together with the printed reference stick. That image is analysed **on your device** to work out width,
height, area, diagonal, and angles. The camera does not record video or send anything in the
background. It is on while Metrologist is open on your screen, including when you move between the
app's screens, and it turns off when you switch to another app or close Metrologist. It starts only after
you grant the camera permission, and a photo is taken only when you tap Capture.

## Your photos stay on your device

- Captured photos are processed **in memory on your device** and are **not uploaded** anywhere.
- The app does **not** save your measurement photos to your gallery or shared storage on its own.
- The image analysis (via the bundled OpenCV library) happens locally. A picture leaves the app only
  when you tap Export and choose where it goes (see "Sharing a result" below).

## Settings you choose

Your preferences — the reference stick's length and width and your preferred display unit (centimetres,
feet and inches, or metres) — are saved in a small private settings file on your own device. The app
never sends this file anywhere, and it is removed from the phone when you uninstall the app or clear
its data. If Android backup is switched on, the file can be copied to your Google backup and restored
from it (see "Device backup" below).

## Sharing a result (only if you ask)

When you choose to share a measurement, the app draws the numbers onto a copy of your photo, saves that
image to its own private cache, and hands it to Android's system share sheet. You pick where it goes — a
messaging app, email, files, and so on. This only happens when you tap Export, and the destination is
chosen entirely by you. The privacy policy of the app you choose applies once it has the image. We never
receive the shared image.

## No internet, no tracking

- The app requests **no internet permission**, so it makes no network connections of its own. Data can leave it only by Export, by the web links on the "About Metrologist" screen (see "External links" below) and, if Android backup is switched on, by Android copying your settings to your Google backup (see "Device backup" below).
- We use **no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising**.
- There are **no third-party tracking SDKs, no cookies, and no advertising identifiers**.
- The only runtime permission the app requests is the **camera** — no location, contacts, microphone, or
  account access.

## Device backup

If you have enabled Android Auto Backup or Google account backup on your device, the operating system
may include this app's local settings in your own personal Google backup. This is controlled entirely by
you and Google — we have no access to it. See
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details.

## External links

The "About Metrologist" screen in the app has buttons that open web pages in your browser: the Metrologist website,
this privacy policy, and the project's GitHub pages (the latest release, the source code and the issue
tracker). A page opens only when you tap its button. The app sends no data with it: it asks Android to
open the address, and your browser then connects to the page.

This website also links to the developer's site ([cocode.dk](https://cocode.dk)) and a LinkedIn profile.
All of these sites are governed by their own privacy policies.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and on the website with a new
"last updated" date.
