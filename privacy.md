# Privacy Policy — Metrologist

**App:** Metrologist (`com.cocode.measureapp`)
**Developer:** CoCode.dk — Babak Bandpey
**Last updated:** 14 July 2026

> The canonical, always-current version of this policy is published at
> **https://cocodedk.github.io/Metrologist/privacy.html**

**Metrologist does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.**
It is an Android app that measures flat surfaces from a photo, and every calculation runs entirely on
your device.

## Camera

Metrologist uses your device camera for one purpose: to photograph the surface you want to measure,
together with the printed reference stick. That image is analysed **on your device** to work out width,
height, area, diagonal, and angles. The camera is never used to record or send anything in the
background — it is active only while you are on the capture screen, and only after you grant the camera
permission.

## Your photos stay on your device

- Captured photos are processed **in memory on your device** and are **not uploaded** anywhere.
- The app does **not** save your measurement photos to your gallery or shared storage on its own.
- The image analysis (via the bundled OpenCV library) happens locally; no image, pixel, or measurement
  ever leaves the app.

## Settings you choose

Your preferences — the reference stick's length and width and your preferred display unit (centimetres,
inches, or metres) — are saved in a small private settings file on your own device. This never leaves
your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

## Sharing a result (only if you ask)

When you choose to share a measurement, the app draws the numbers onto a copy of your photo, saves that
image to its own private cache, and hands it to Android's system share sheet. You pick where it goes — a
messaging app, email, files, and so on. This only happens when you tap share, and the destination is
chosen entirely by you. We never receive the shared image.

## No internet, no tracking

- The app requests **no internet permission**, so it is technically unable to send your data anywhere.
- We use **no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising**.
- There are **no third-party tracking SDKs, no cookies, and no advertising identifiers**.
- The only runtime permission the app requests is the **camera** — no location, contacts, microphone, or
  account access.

## Device backup

If you have enabled Android Auto Backup or Google account backup on your device, the operating system
may include this app's local settings in your own personal Google backup. This is controlled entirely by
you and Google — we have no access to it. See
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details.

## External links

The app and this website link to the developer's site ([cocode.dk](https://cocode.dk)), a LinkedIn
profile, and the project's GitHub page. Selecting them opens your browser; those sites are governed by
their own privacy policies.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.
