Measure flat surfaces from one photo · Android · Free

Measure with your phone.
One photo. Real-world sizes.

Lay a reference stick of known size on a flat surface and photograph it in the app. Drag one box onto the four corners of the object and another onto the four corners of the stick. Metrologist estimates the width, height, area, diagonal and corner angles. It works without internet.

How it works

Three steps from photo to measurement

STEP 01

Photograph the surface

Lay the reference stick, with its red ends and checker border, flat on the same surface as the wall, door, window, floor or table you want to measure. Take the photo in the app, in even light. Straight on is fine, but avoid nearly edge-on views.

STEP 02

Mark corners + stick

Before your first measurement, enter the stick's length and width in Settings. Then drag the green box's corners onto the four corners of the object, and the red box's corners onto the four outside corners of the stick. Zoom with two fingers, and use the magnifier that appears while you drag.

STEP 03

Read the results

The result shows the width, height, area, diagonal and corner angles, with a confidence score and, when relevant, notes. You need no account, and the app has no internet permission.

Features

What Metrologist does

Real-world units

Results in centimetres, metres, or feet and inches. Choose your units in Settings.

Works offline

All calculation happens on your phone. The app has no internet permission, and you need no account.

Confidence score

Each result shows a confidence label (High, Medium or Low) and, when there is something to point out, notes about the photo. It does not guarantee the size is right, so read the notes and check important sizes.

Walls, floors, doors, windows

Works on a flat, four-cornered surface such as a wall, door, window, floor or table top. The app handles the perspective; you drag the boxes onto the corners.

Zoom and magnifier

Zoom with two fingers. A magnifier appears while you drag a corner, so you can place it precisely.

Tested measurement engine

The measurement engine has automated tests that use synthetic scenes. They do not show how accurate it is with a real phone camera, so check important sizes with a ruler or tape.

Accuracy

How close does it get?

Estimates
Not certified measurements
Example: 92%

Metrologist works out sizes from one photograph, with no laser and no tape. How close the result is depends on your phone, the photo, the viewing angle and how carefully you place the reference stick and the corners. Treat every result as an estimate. Before you cut materials, order supplies or quote a job, check the sizes that matter with a ruler or tape. The confidence score and the notes under the result show when it is worth taking the photo again.
