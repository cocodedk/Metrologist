Metrologist estimates the dimensions of a flat surface from a photo taken inside the app. Place a reference stick of known length and width on the same plane, then mark the four corners of the object and the four outside corners of the reference.

Features:
• Camera capture with tilt information
• Wall and floor/table surface modes
• Adjustable corner markers, zoom and a magnifier
• Width, height, area, diagonal and corner-angle results
• Confidence indicators and measurement diagnostics
• Metric and imperial display units
• Export an annotated image through Android sharing

Processing happens on your device. The app requests camera access and has no ads or analytics.

Use a flat reference with accurately measured dimensions, clear lighting and visible corners. Results are estimates, not certified measurements. Verify important dimensions with a ruler or tape measure.
