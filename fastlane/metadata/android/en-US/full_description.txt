Metrologist estimates the width, height, area, diagonal and corner angles of a flat surface, such as a wall, door, window, floor or table, from a photo you take in the app. Lay a reference stick of known length and width on the same surface. Then drag one box onto the four corners of the object and another onto the four outside corners of the stick.

What you get:
• A camera that records the phone's tilt with each photo
• Wall and Floor modes, which the app often picks for you from the tilt
• Corner handles you can drag, zoom with two fingers, and a magnifier
• Width, height, area, diagonal and corner angles
• A confidence score and notes on every result
• Results in centimetres, metres, or feet and inches
• Export: share the photo with the measurements written on it, through the Android share sheet

All calculation happens on your phone. The app asks only for camera permission, and it has no internet permission, no ads and no analytics.

Use a flat reference stick whose length and width you have measured, good light and a view where all corners can be seen. Results are estimates, not certified measurements. Check important sizes with a ruler or tape measure.

User guide and printable reference stick: https://measure.cocode.dk/user-guide.html
